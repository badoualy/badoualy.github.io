1. What Tsukiji does

Tsukiji lets you:

  • browse kanji, vocabulary, radicals and kana, with readings, meanings and stroke order;
  • study with quizzes and spaced-repetition reviews, and track your progress;
  • create custom study groups;
  • hear words read aloud through your device’s text-to-speech engine;
  • receive an optional daily reminder notification.

Your progress and settings are stored only on your device; see the Privacy Policy.

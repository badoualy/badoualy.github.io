Tsukiji — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Last updated: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what personal data the Tsukiji Android application (the “App”, “we”, “us”), an app for learning Japanese kanji, vocabulary and kana, processes and why. The App is published by Yannick Badoual, an individual developer based in France, who is the data controller within the meaning of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”).

Quick summary. Tsukiji has no account, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. Your study progress, custom groups, searches and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us. The only data that leaves your device is a technical crash report, sent to Google’s Firebase Crashlytics service when the App crashes, so that we can fix the bug. We do not sell your data.

1. Who we are & how to contact us

Data controller: Yannick Badoual, individual developer, France (full publisher details in the Mentions Légales).
Contact for any privacy request: tsukiji.app.support@gmail.com

If you live in the EU/EEA, you can also lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. In France, this is the Commission Nationale de l’Informatique et des Libertés (CNIL — cnil.fr).

2. What data the App handles

2.1 Data stored only on your device

To work, the App saves the following in its private storage on your phone. This data is not transmitted to us or to any third party by the App, and we have no access to it:

  • study progress: for each kanji, word or kana you study, its learning status, review schedule and correct/incorrect counts;
  • answer history: the answers you give in quizzes and reviews (item, chosen answer, whether it was correct, response time, date and time), the items you tend to confuse, and daily study statistics;
  • custom groups you create (name, colour, icon, the items they contain);
  • your last 8 searches (you can clear them from the search screen);
  • your settings (theme, display options, daily goals, reminder on/off and time, focus set) and your last quiz configuration.

The kanji, vocabulary and stroke-order content is a read-only dataset bundled inside the App; looking it up does not involve any network request.

2.2 Crash data (Firebase Crashlytics)

The released version of the App includes Firebase Crashlytics, a Google service that notifies us when the App crashes so we can fix it. When a crash happens, Crashlytics collects and sends to Google’s servers:

  • the crash stack trace (the technical location of the error in the App’s code) and the state of the App’s threads at that moment;
  • device information: brand, model, Android version, and technical state such as available memory/storage, orientation and whether the device is rooted;
  • app information: app version and the date and time of the crash;
  • a randomly generated installation identifier (Crashlytics installation UUID / Firebase installation ID) — not your name or email — which lets us count how many installations are affected by a given crash;
  • app-session start events, which Crashlytics uses to compute crash-free statistics;
  • your IP address, which Google’s servers necessarily receive when the report is transmitted.

Crash reports do not contain your study progress, answers, groups or searches. We do not attach any custom user identifier or log to them. Crash data is used solely for stability monitoring and debugging — not for advertising or profiling.

2.3 Device features the App uses

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): only if you turn on the daily reminder. The reminder is scheduled and displayed locally on your device; no push service or server is involved. The App also re-schedules it after a device restart, time change or app update (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED).
  • Text-to-speech: when you tap to hear a word, its Japanese text is passed to the text-to-speech engine installed on your device (for example Google’s). That engine is provided by a third party and governed by its own privacy policy; the App sends nothing else.
  • Clipboard: the App only writes to the clipboard when you tap “copy” on a kanji. It does not read your clipboard; the paste box of the group editor only receives text you paste into it yourself.
  • Links: the contact, privacy and terms links open your email app or browser.

2.4 Data we do not collect

  • No account, name, email address, phone number or payment information.
  • No access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • No advertising, attribution or analytics SDK.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal data.

4. Who has access to your data (processors)

  • Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC — Firebase Crashlytics, acting as our processor under the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms. See also Privacy and security in Firebase and the Google Privacy Policy.

Apart from this processor, we do not share your personal data with any third party, except where strictly required by law or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Google Play, through which you install the App, processes data under its own terms as an independent controller.

5. International transfers

Crash data may be processed by Google in the United States and other countries where Google operates. These transfers rely on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses incorporated in Google’s data processing terms and, where applicable, on Google’s certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

6. How long data is kept

Category Retention
On-device data (progress, history, groups, searches, settings) Until you delete it: clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App erases it (subject to your own Android backup, see section 7).
Crash reports (Crashlytics) Up to 90 days for individual crash reports and their installation identifiers, per Google’s Firebase documentation; aggregated, non-identifying stability metrics may be kept longer.

7. Android backup

The App allows Android’s standard backup. If backup is enabled on your device, Android may include the App’s data (progress, history, groups, searches, settings) in the backup it stores in your own Google account, and may copy it when you transfer to a new phone. This backup is operated by Google under your Google account settings; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your device settings (typically Settings → System → Backup).

8. Your rights & choices

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. To exercise them, write to tsukiji.app.support@gmail.com. Because the App has no account, we cannot link a crash report to you unless you help us identify the installation concerned.

  • Delete your on-device data: Settings → Apps → Tsukiji → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the App. This also resets the installation identifier used by Crashlytics.
  • Stop crash reporting: the App does not currently offer an in-app switch; you can object by contacting us, or stop all future reports by uninstalling the App.
  • Reminders: turn the reminder off in the App’s settings or revoke the notification permission in Android settings.

9. Security

On-device data is kept in the App’s private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Crash reports are sent to Google over an encrypted (TLS) connection. No system is 100% secure; in the event of a personal data breach likely to result in a risk to your rights, we will notify the CNIL within 72 hours (Art. 33 GDPR) and, where required, inform affected users (Art. 34 GDPR).

10. Children

Tsukiji is an educational app for a general audience and can be used by learners of any age. It does not ask for any personal information and does not include ads, chat or social features. The only data leaving the device is the technical crash data described in section 2.2. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question or request about your child’s data, contact us at tsukiji.app.support@gmail.com.

11. U.S. state privacy rights (e.g. California)

We do not sell personal information and do not “share” it for cross-context behavioural advertising. The only categories processed are device identifiers and technical crash information, used for the purpose described above. You may request access or deletion by contacting us; we will not discriminate against you for doing so.

5. Reporting Illegal Content

Under Article 6.I.5 of the LCEN and Article 16 of the EU Digital Services Act (Regulation (EU) 2022/2065), any user can notify us of content within the App that they believe is illegal — for example defamatory text in a trip note, an image they hold rights to that was uploaded without permission, or content that violates third-party privacy.

Notifications must be sent to splitzy.app.support@gmail.com and include:

  • the date of the notification;
  • your full name, postal address, email address (and, if you are a legal entity, your form, registered office and legal representative);
  • a precise description of the disputed content and its location in the App (trip ID, expense ID, etc.);
  • the legal grounds on which the content should be removed;
  • a copy of any prior correspondence with the author of the content asking for its withdrawal, or a justification of why no such correspondence could take place;
  • a statement that the information in the notification is accurate, made in good faith.

We will acknowledge receipt without undue delay and act on the notification with the diligence required by the LCEN and the DSA. Submitting a manifestly abusive notification is a criminal offence under Article 6.I.4 of the LCEN.
